# 03 Stakeholders (P2P)

**TL;DR**
- All roles below are standard P2P roles [INFERRED]; LYB's actual org is "Data unavailable — requires mentor validation."
- Three-role demo (supplier, AP clerk, approver) maps to the most affected roles.
- Highest-power: finance controllers, internal audit, DT/security, ERP admins.
- Resistance risk: approvers (accountability), ERP admins (new silo), clerks (fear of replacement).
- Verified LYB facts: buyers are suppliers' primary contact; AP handles account status (S2).
