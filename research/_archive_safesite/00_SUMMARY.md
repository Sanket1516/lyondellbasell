# 00 Summary — SafeSite Research

**TL;DR**
- Real problem: safety information is lost between noticing a hazard and fixing it.
- Primary user: contractor/new joiner (default); constraints max priority (team-confirmed).
- Evidence is strong on barriers & La Porte; weak on LYB's actual process and tools.
- Research is enough to start solution discovery, with 3 mentor questions open.

**Problem (3 lines):** At fictional Plant X, hazards/near-misses go unreported; contractors and new joiners are most at risk. Build a mobile-first app for <1-minute reporting, routing, closed-loop actions and a risk heatmap. Scored on 10 criteria; prototype 20, impact 15.

**Top 10 insights**
1. Fear of blame and no visible follow-up drive under-reporting (S7). 2. Near-miss reporting is mostly voluntary, not mandated (S7). 3. ISO 45001 expects barrier removal (language, shifts, fear) and root-cause action (S8). 4. La Porte: contractors, "simple" task, no procedure/training (S5). 5. LYB's GoalZERO and contractor HSE requirement exist (S1–S3). 6. No LYB EHS system is public [UNKNOWN]. 7. Competitors offer mobile/offline/CAPA; AI triage is a differentiator (S10). 8. Closure loop (owner, due, escalation) is the core gap [INFERRED]. 9. Reporting may rise first (not worse safety). 10. DPDP applies to worker data/photos (S11).

**Three most painful experiences (illustrative):** worker fears job loss for reporting; same hazard reported repeatedly with no response; hazard info lost at shift handover.

**Five constraints:** gloves/noise/restricted phones; offline; multilingual; AI misclassification (human review needed); privacy (photos, location, identity).

**Five opportunity areas:** contractor-friendly voice/icon reporting; visible feedback loop; owner+due+escalation by default; shift-handover repeat-hazard view; advisory AI with human override.

**Top 3 unanswered questions:** judging weight/success definition; LYB hazard & severity taxonomy; ownership/escalation/closure rules ([10](10_questions_for_mentors.md)).
