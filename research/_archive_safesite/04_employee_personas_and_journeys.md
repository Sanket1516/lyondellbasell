# 04 Employee Personas & Journeys

**TL;DR**
- ALL names, quotes, locations, timelines are ILLUSTRATIVE fictions built from verified barriers (S5, S7, S8); none are LYB employees or interviews.
- Primary persona (Q1 default): A, Contract Worker. Constraints (Q2 confirmed): gloves, noise, offline, restricted phones, multilingual.
- Biggest pain moments: deciding whether it's "worth" reporting; fear of consequence; never hearing back.
- Highest-leverage moments: first 30 seconds at the hazard, and the feedback after submission.
- Everything needs mentor validation ([10](10_questions_for_mentors.md)).

## Persona A — Contract Worker (PRIMARY) — "Ravi Patil" (illustrative)
Role: scaffolding/fitter via third-party contractor · Location: Plant X, Unit 2 shutdown area · Experience: 3 yrs overall, 2 weeks on this site · Language: Marathi/Hindi, basic English.
Responsibilities: execute assigned job under permit; follow foreman. Goals: finish job, get paid, stay hired. Frustrations: unfamiliar zone names; rules in English; feels his report may cost him the contract; no phone in process area. Tech: smartphone WhatsApp-fluent, little desktop. Comms: voice, icons, foreman. Quote (illustrative): *"If I say something, will they send me home?"*
Barriers [INFERRED from S5/S7/S8]: fear of reprisal; language; unfamiliar zone names; task perceived as routine (S5: La Porte task viewed as simple, no procedure). Workaround: tells foreman verbally; nothing recorded. Impact: hazard stays, no trace. Moment of difference: one-tap/voice report in own language with confidential option, plus "your report was received/acted on".

## Persona B — New Plant Operator — "Aisha Khan" (illustrative)
Employee, 4 months, Control room/field operator, Unit 1. Goals: learn, be trusted. Frustrations: can't tell normal from hazardous; induction was a day of slides; hesitates to escalate to seniors. Tech: high. Comms: asks buddy, chat. Quote: *"I wasn't sure it was a real problem, so I didn't want to bother anyone."* Need: in-context guidance and examples of what to report.

## Persona C — Experienced Operator — "Suresh Nair" (illustrative)
18 yrs, field operator, Utilities. Goals: smooth shift. Frustrations: same leaking flange reported verbally for months; reporting fatigue; normalised risk. Tech: moderate. Comms: logbook/handover/verbal. Quote: *"We've known about it forever; telling them again changes nothing."* Need: visible closure and repeat-hazard acknowledgement.

## Persona D — Shift Supervisor — "Meena Joshi" (illustrative)
10 yrs, shift supervisor. Goals: safe, on-schedule shift. Frustrations: info overload; follow-ups lost at handover; can't see patterns. Tech: high. Comms: radio, handover notes. Quote: *"I get ten things at once; I need to know which one can hurt someone today."* Need: prioritised queue, one-tap assign with due date, handover view.

## Persona E — HSE / Safety Manager — "Vikram Rao" (illustrative)
15 yrs, HSE manager. Goals: trends, closure, audit readiness. Frustrations: manual compilation, late investigations, can't prove closure. Tech: high; Excel/BI. Quote: *"I can't fix what I can't see, and I can't prove what I fixed."* Need: aggregate by zone/shift, overdue/escalated actions, audit trail.
