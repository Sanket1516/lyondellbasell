# 03 Stakeholders

**TL;DR**
- All entries are [ASSUMPTION]/[INFERRED] role models, not LYB-verified org facts.
- Core lifecycle stakeholders: contractor, new joiner, operator, shift supervisor, HSE officer/manager, plant manager.
- Gatekeepers for adoption: contractor-management/HR, security/site access, DT/IT.
- Highest risk of resistance: supervisors (extra workload/blame), contractor firms (liability fear).
- Excluded as unrelated: regulators/auditors only as downstream consumers.

## Power–interest grid
- High power / high interest (manage closely): HSE manager, plant manager, DT.
- High power / lower interest (keep satisfied): regulators/auditors, site leadership.
- Low power / high interest (keep informed — but they are the users): contract workers, new joiners, supervisors.
- Low/low: security, emergency response (inform).
Design note [INFERRED]: users with least power are the ones whose adoption decides success.
