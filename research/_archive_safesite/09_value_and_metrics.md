## KPIs
| Level | Metric | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Worker | Median time to submit (target hypothesis <60 s per brief) | Leading |
| Worker | Contractor share of reports; % reports with photo+zone | Leading |
| Supervisor | Time to acknowledge; time to assign; overdue actions; escalation rate | Leading |
| Safety mgmt | Near-miss frequency; repeat-hazard rate; hotspots by zone/shift; closure rate/age | Leading |
| Business | Admin hours; audit readiness; avoided incident cost | Lagging/estimated |
Leading = predicts/prevents (reports, closure speed); lagging = outcomes (injuries, incidents).
