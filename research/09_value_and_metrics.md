# 09 Value & Metrics

**TL;DR**
- All figures are illustrative with stated assumptions; no LYB baselines exist ("Data unavailable — requires mentor validation").
- Pick one headline metric for the pitch (impact = 15 pts): hours saved on exceptions or duplicate payments prevented.
- Benchmarks are vendor-reported ranges (S9, S12); use "reported by vendor" wording.
- Avoid double counting: time saved and cost saved are the same benefit.
- Working-capital benefit is separate and only claim with explicit terms assumptions.

| KPI | Definition / measurement | Why | Data needed | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Processing time | receipt → posted (median) | Speed | timestamps | Cash cycle |
| Cost/invoice | total AP cost ÷ invoices | Efficiency | cost data | Savings |
| First-pass match | % auto-matched without touch | Automation | match log | Fewer touches |
| Manual intervention | % with human edit | Effort | audit log | FTE time |
| Exception resolution time | open → closed | Responsiveness | exception log | Late payments |
| Duplicate detection | flagged ÷ true duplicates | Control | labelled data | Cash leakage |
| Duplicate payment prevention | blocked amount | Control | — | Cash |
| Approval turnaround | submit → decision | Bottleneck | timestamps | Delay |
| On-time payment % | paid by due date | Supplier trust | due/paid dates | Penalties |
| Supplier inquiry volume | status queries | Workload | ticket count | Hours |
| Audit prep time | hours per audit | Compliance | — | Hours |

## Illustrative calc (assumptions, fictional)
Exceptions: 2,000 invoices/month × 15% exceptions = 300 exceptions; saving 20 min each = 100 h/month = 1,200 h/yr. Status queries: 500/month × 5 min deflected = ~42 h/month. No salary assigned. If 1,000 invoices/year have value ₹X and 0.5% duplicates blocked → 0.005 × ₹X (assumption).
Working capital: only claim if payment terms (e.g. 45 d, S4) and early-pay discounts are known.
